# Figma prompt — TameerBook

Paste everything below the line into Figma (Figma Make / First Draft / any AI design tool).
It describes only the problem, the people, and what they need to do — the design is yours to make.

---

Design a mobile app called **TameerBook** for small property builders, plot investors and
contractors in Pakistan. Here is the problem it exists to solve.

## Who these people are

A builder or plot investor, 35–55, in Lahore or Rawalpindi. They buy a plot, build a house or
shops on it, and sell it — often with two or three partners putting up the money. They run deals
worth crores. They work on site, on a cheap Android phone, one-handed, in sunlight. They read
slowly, some barely read at all. They think in *kharcha* (expense), *aamdani* (income), *bayana*
(down payment), *dehari* (daily wage), *marla* and *kanal* — never in "debit", "credit" or
"ledger". They have never used a finance app and will abandon anything that looks like
accounting software. Many work where there is no signal, so the app is fully offline: no login,
no cloud, no internet.

## What their life looks like today

Everything lives in a paper register, a bundle of receipt slips in a drawer, and WhatsApp
messages. Because of that:

- **Nobody knows how much money they actually have.** Cash is spread across cash in hand, two
  bank accounts and a mobile wallet at once, and nothing adds them up.
- **Plot money and construction money get mixed together.** A plot is paid for in stages — token,
  then bayana, then instalments — plus transfer tax and the dealer's commission. Then cement,
  sariya, bricks and daily wages are paid on top for months. When it's over, nobody can say what
  the unit really cost, so nobody knows what price is actually a profit.
- **Partner accounts are guesswork.** Several investors fund one flip. Who put in how much, whose
  capital is still outstanding, what each one's share of the profit should be — all of it gets
  reconstructed from memory on the day the property sells. That is exactly when arguments start,
  and these are relationships that have to survive the next deal too.
- **Daily-wage labour is tracked on a wall.** For each worker, each day: full day, half day, or
  absent. A running balance builds up, part payments come off it, and the whole record is one
  lost page away from a dispute with the worker.
- **Money lent out quietly disappears.** Cash handed to a relative, a dealer or a labourer is
  remembered rather than recorded, and often never comes back.
- **They can't show anything to anyone.** No statement to hand a partner, no receipt for a
  seller, no cost sheet for a buyer, no proof for anyone who asks.

## What the app has to let them do

- See, on opening the app, **how much money exists and where it is** — every account, and the
  total worth of the business including plots held and money already sunk into construction.
- **Record a payment in seconds, on site**, with the date already set to today and the likely
  category and account already chosen — plus a photo of the receipt slip.
- Keep **each plot as its own file** carrying every rupee it cost, its size and location, its
  seller, its papers, and whether it is still held, under construction, or sold.
- Treat **a project as: a plot + construction + a sale** — so the true cost and the true profit
  of a deal are always visible while it is still running, not only at the end.
- Keep **each partner's capital and profit share calculated, not remembered**, including how much
  of their pledge is still free to put into the next deal, and settle everyone's share — plus the
  charity portion they set aside — in one clear final reckoning.
- Mark **attendance for daily-wage workers** day by day, accrue what is owed, and pay it off in
  parts.
- Track **money lent out and money borrowed**, per person, until it comes back.
- Track **orders placed with material suppliers** and what is still unpaid.
- Produce **something printable to hand to a partner, a seller or a buyer** — statements and
  receipts that look like the ruled notebook ledger they already trust.
- Never lose history: a mistake is corrected by a reversal entry, never by deleting the record.
- Work entirely in **English or Urdu**, on a small, cheap screen, for someone who reads slowly.

The core promise: **one book in the pocket that always knows where the money is, what each deal
really cost, and what each partner is owed.**
